# Human CAPTCHA

> **You've been online too long. Prove you still exist.**

A tiny anti-doomscroll experiment that flips the CAPTCHA idea around.

Normal CAPTCHA:
> prove you're not a robot

Human CAPTCHA:
> prove you're still a person

It gives you small reality checks like:

- what were you doing before opening this tab?
- name something you can hear in the real world
- what day is it?
- take a 10-second screen break
- identify your next useful action

Then it calculates an extremely scientific **Humanity Score™**.

## Why

This is intentionally silly on the surface, but the interaction is built around a real idea:

**interrupt automatic scrolling with tiny moments of conscious recall.**

No accounts.  
No backend.  
No tracking.  
No fake medical claims.

Just a small browser experiment with too much personality.
